## 🎬 Video Storyboard & Script Outline (3 Minutes)

### [00:00 - 00:30] Introduction & Enterprise Positioning
- **Visual:** Open `http://localhost:8080` showing the **StoryLensLab InterneersLab Console**.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Hello judges! We are Team Dunhill from IIT Kanpur. Traditional business intelligence tools tell you *that* revenue dropped. AI dashboards generate ungrounded chat responses. 
  > 
  > StoryLens AI 2.0 is an evidence-first, governed decision intelligence platform. It establishes an auditable enterprise loop: **Detect, Investigate, Challenge, Decide, Act, Measure, and Learn**."

---

### [00:30 - 01:15] Investigation & Hypothesis Falsification
- **Visual:** Click **Overview** showing active incident `Net Revenue is ₹8.1M below expected performance (-8.1%)`. Switch to **Investigation**.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Notice our active incident: Revenue is ₹8.1M below expected performance. StoryLens reconciles SAP, WMS, and Zendesk data in 153 milliseconds. 
  > 
  > But StoryLens doesn't just stop at identifying Supplier A as the primary driver. It actively challenges its own conclusion through our **Hypothesis Falsification Engine**—asking *'What evidence would change my mind?'*. For example, if competitor pricing drops over 12%, confidence degrades by 18 points."

---

### [01:15 - 02:00] Driver Waterfall & Monte-Carlo Decision Simulator
- **Visual:** Click **Driver Analysis** showing Price-Volume-Mix Waterfall chart, then click **Decision Lab**. Slide the volume reallocation slider to 22%.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "In Driver Analysis, our Price-Volume-Mix waterfall proves cancellations account for ₹2.4M of the revenue gap. 
  > 
  > In Decision Lab, we simulate reallocating 22% of Supplier A outbound volume to Supplier B. StoryLens runs a **500-trial Monte-Carlo simulation** across demand, transport costs, and capacity, confirming an **87% Decision Robustness Score** with an expected recovery range of ₹2.6M to ₹3.4M."

---

### [02:00 - 02:30] Closed-Loop Outcome Tracking & Decision Lineage
- **Visual:** Scroll down in **Decision Lab** to show **Closed-Loop Recommendation Outcome (+28 Days)** and **Decision Lineage Log**.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Crucially, StoryLens closes the action loop. +28 days after executing the intervention, StoryLens tracks observed outcomes—recording ₹2.72M actual recovery (-10.2% prediction error) and updating future simulation weights. 
  > 
  > Through Decision Memory, future similar incidents automatically retrieve this successful resolution."

---

### [02:30 - 03:00] Executive Brief & Governance Audit
- **Visual:** Click **📄 Executive Decision Brief** modal button, then switch to **Governance & Audit**.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "With a single click, StoryLens generates a C-Suite Executive Decision Brief with Level 3 Autonomy governance, risk assessment, and decision SLA deadlines. 
  > 
  > Finally, in Governance & Audit, our CFO-signed Trusted Analytics Registry and 42-incident ground-truth benchmark verify zero numeric hallucinations and 100% claim fidelity. Thank you!"
